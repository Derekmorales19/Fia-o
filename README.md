# FIA´O

## 1. About the Project

FIA´O is a Panamanian digital platform created to modernize credit or "on-account" management in Panama's mini-marts.

## 2. What Do We Do?

FIA´O provides a digital solution that allows mini-markets to manage their daily operations more efficiently.

### Main Areas
- Credits or Fiados
- Inventory
- Sales

## 3. Main Objective

Our objective is to reduce errors, optimize business management, and transform traditional credit management into a safer, faster, and more responsible experience.

## 4. System Sections and Functionalities

### 4.1. Home Page
- Main landing page.
- Presents the value proposition: "Your credit notebook, now digital."
- Login and Sign Up buttons.
- Navigation menu.
- Language selector (ES).
- Light and Dark theme selectors.

### 4.2. Dashboard
- Main control panel for the store owner.
- General overview of the business and its activities.

### 4.3. Credits / Fiados
- Display total outstanding debt.
- Show payments received today.
- Show clients with outstanding debt.
- Identify overdue credits.
- Register new credits.
- Record partial or full payments.
- Mark debts as paid.
- Filter credits by status.
- View credit history by client.
- Activate automatic blocking when a client exceeds their credit limit.

### 4.4. Clients
- Register clients with name, phone, optional email, credit limit, and notes.
- Filter clients by status.
- Sort clients by name or outstanding debt.
- Send personalized payment reminders through WhatsApp or email.

### 4.5. Products and Inventory

#### Inventory
- Register and manage products.
- Organize products by categories.
- Manage prices, stock, and units of measurement.
- Configure minimum stock alerts.
- Identify products with low or zero stock.
- Calculate total inventory value.

#### Register Fiado Sale
- Add products to a credit sale cart.
- Select inventory products or manual items.
- Verify the client's credit limit before confirming a sale.

#### Stock Movements
- Track inventory entries and exits.
- Register stock adjustments and sales.
- Maintain an inventory audit log.

### 4.6. Reports
- Filter information by date, client, and status.
- Display total credits issued.
- Show payments received.
- Calculate pending and overdue balances.
- Display average amount per credit.
- Visualize business activity and payment statuses.
- Show clients with the highest outstanding debts.
- Export reports in PDF and Excel formats.

### 4.7. Contact / Support
- AI-powered chat assistant.
- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs).
- Contact form.
- Official support channels.
- Support email and service hours.

### 4.8. My Profile / Settings
- Manage account information.
- Configure personal preferences.

### 4.9. About Us
- Information about the project.
- Project purpose and background.
